Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This website is run by Muhammad Ali, trading as Real Editor (“I”, “me”). This page explains what information is collected when you visit therealeditor.netlify.app and when you contact me, and how it is used.

Information you send me

This website has no sign-up forms. If you contact me on WhatsApp or by email, I receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address, social media handles, and any videos or files you send. I use this only to reply to you, give you a quote and do the work you ask for.

The chat assistant

The “Ask Real Editor” chat on this website runs in your browser. It gives pre-written answers and does not store or send your messages anywhere. If you choose to continue on WhatsApp, the message is sent through WhatsApp, not this website.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the hosting provider (Netlify) records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you open, to keep the website secure and working. Fonts on this website are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers.

Analytics and cookies

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful, for example how many people open a page or tap the WhatsApp button. Google Analytics only runs if you click “Accept” on the cookie banner. If you click “Decline”, no analytics cookies are set.

Google Analytics uses cookies (such as _ga) and collects information like the pages you visit, roughly where you are (country or city), your device and browser. I can’t see who you are from this data. It is processed by Google under its own privacy policy.

Your choice is saved in your browser so the banner doesn’t keep showing. You can change it any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page.

Third-party services

When you contact me, your messages are handled by the service you choose: WhatsApp (Meta) or Gmail (Google). Their own privacy policies apply to those messages. Your video files may be shared with me through services such as Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer.

How long I keep your information

I keep your messages and files only as long as needed to finish your project and handle any follow-up questions. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

I don’t sell your information

I never sell or rent your personal information, and I only share it with services needed to do your project.

Your rights

You can ask me what information I have about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Just send a message to the contact details below.
